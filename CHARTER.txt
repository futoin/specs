3. "FutoIn", "FutoIn Trust", "FutoIn Project" and "FutoIn Foundation" can be

4. The Founder is rightful to represent the organization without limits, adjust

8. FutoIn respects the original authorship of its projects and discourages any
   type of disgrace or disrespect of the original ideas and intentions with
